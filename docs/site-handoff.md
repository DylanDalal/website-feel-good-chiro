# Feel Good Chiropractic — completed website

The approved pink sunset direction is now the application, not just a standalone concept. The first screen pairs the unchanged “Get back to feeling good.” headline with two rounded clinical photographs and a pink overlay. There is no video or motion toggle; scroll-linked scenery respects reduced-motion preferences.

## Pages

- Home, care overview, doctor profile, first visit, contact, patient perspectives, and website privacy.
- Six treatment pages: chiropractic, acupuncture, Cox flexion-distraction, sports/rehabilitation, exercise recovery and sauna, and soft tissue care.
- Seven concern pages: back, neck, sciatica, knee, shoulder, joint/hip, and foot/ankle.
- Thirteen retained educational articles with a redesigned topic index.
- Shared navigation, mobile menu, mobile call/booking links, and missing-page responses.

The site has 33 indexable content routes. Treatments and condition pages link to related care; the home page and care overview connect the full content set.

## Search and migration

Every content route has a distinct title, description, canonical URL, one primary heading, and crawlable text. The sitemap and robots files reference the intended production domain, feelgoodchiro.net. Business, doctor, article, and care-page breadcrumb structured data describe visible facts. There are no meta-keyword lists, hidden keyword blocks, artificial neighborhood office pages, or self-serving review ratings.

Permanent redirects preserve the legacy article and care paths that changed: forms, office, decompression, joint problems, massage, sports physicals, age-specific care, and the old root-level articles. Main back-pain, knee-pain, neck-pain, acupuncture, foot-care, chiropractic-care, about-us, reviews, and contact paths remain available.

The original domain is not switched by publishing to Sites. The Sites project's existing owner-only audience is preserved. Before the public-domain cutover, confirm practice details, clinical copy, existing photo usage rights, and domain/hosting access. Review the old legal pages and any current promotion before migrating them; they are not automatically replaced with unrelated redirects. This build has no medical intake form or analytics/tracking pixels.
